01 / Overview

Immersion as an embodied process.

A VR interactive experience about embodiment: through spatial layout, bodily movement, and interaction design, it shifts VR away from mind-centric control toward physical awareness.

Immersion becomes participation and acceptance rather than navigation or achievement. The journey moves through two contrasting spaces:

HighLand The River
Movement Uphill terrain, falling obstacles, elevated puzzles, vertical climbing Seated, rhythmic hand-pedaling
Space Vast; the player is small against its scale A compressed corridor of pillars that guides attention forward
The body Brought into awareness through strain and repetition Agency shifts fully into the body; movement as flow
Feeling Resistance and effort Being watched: eyes in the environment reverse the gaze
02 / HighLand

A space of resistance and effort.

Uphill terrain, falling obstacles, elevated puzzles, and vertical climbing gradually bring the body into awareness. Movement takes persistence, balance, and labor, and the player is small against the scale of the environment. Embodiment emerges through strain and repetition rather than mastery.

03 / The River

Movement as flow rather than struggle.

A compressed corridor of pillars guides attention forward. Seated, rhythmic hand-pedaling generates motion, shifting agency fully into the body. Eyes embedded in the environment reverse the gaze, so the player feels observed as they move quietly toward enclosure and release.
